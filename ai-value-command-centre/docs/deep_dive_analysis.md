## Part 1. HPE's AI business: what the public record shows

> PUBLIC data only. Annual figures are from HPE's [FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1645590/000164559025000130/hpe-20251031.htm) (fiscal years end 31 October); FY2026 quarterly figures are from the 10-Qs and 8-K earnings releases ([Q3 FY2026 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1645590/000164559026000078/ex-991x922026x8k.htm)). AI order, backlog and customer figures are company-reported KPIs from earnings calls and slides; HPE describes them as unaudited. Ratios marked *calculated* are this analysis's arithmetic on those figures.

### 1.1 Scale and trajectory

| $M | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total net revenue | 29,135 | 30,127 | 34,296 |
| Gross profit | 10,239 | 9,878 | 10,377 |
| Gross margin (*calculated*) | 35.1% | 32.8% | 30.3% |

| Earnings (loss) from operations | 2,089 | 2,190 | -437 |
| Net earnings attributable to HPE | 2,025 | 2,579 | 57 |
| Operating cash flow | 4,428 | 4,341 | 2,919 |
| Free cash flow (HPE non-GAAP) | 2,238 | 2,297 | 986 |

* Revenue grew 13.8% in FY2025 (*calculated*), helped by about four months of Juniper Networks after the acquisition closed on 2 July 2025 [AI-31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1645590/000114036125024519/ef20051378_ex99-1.htm). Gross margin fell from 35.1% to 30.3% over the two years (*calculated*). The 10-K explains the FY2025 fall in Server gross profit as due to "input cost increases and higher mix of lower margin products".
* The FY2025 operating loss of $437.0M includes $1,621M of impairment charges (10-K). Free cash flow fell to $986.0M.
* FY2026 has turned sharply: revenue of $9.3B, $10.7B and $12.2B in Q1-Q3 ($32.2B for nine months, already 94% of FY2025's full year, *calculated*). Q3 gross margin was 40.1% and operating profit $1.4B. HPE guides FY2026 revenue growth of 34-37% (Q3 release). The 10-Q attributes part of Q3 server growth to higher selling prices from memory and SSD cost inflation [AI-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1645590/000164559026000080/hpe-20260731.htm), so not all of the growth is volume.

### 1.3 AI demand: orders, backlog and conversion

* **Backlog.** AI systems backlog reached $6.8B exiting Q3 FY2026, up 14% sequentially [AI-03](https://investors.hpe.com/~/media/Files/H/HP-Enterprise-IR/documents/q3-2026/q3-2026-earnings-presentation.pdf); including Networks for AI, total AI backlog was a record $7.6B, with $3.1B of AI orders in the quarter and $6.7B year to date [AI-04](https://investors.hpe.com/~/media/Files/H/HP-Enterprise-IR/documents/q3-2026/q3-2026-earnings-presentation.pdf). The slide series runs from $2.4B at Q4 FY2024.
* **Revenue.** AI systems revenue was "almost $1.6 billion" in Q3 FY2026 [AI-02](https://investors.hpe.com/~/media/Files/H/HP-Enterprise-IR/documents/q3-2026/q3-2026-transcript.pdf). Against Cloud & AI revenue of $9.0B that is about 18% of the segment (*calculated*), and the $6.8B backlog equals about 4.2 quarters of AI systems revenue at the Q3 run-rate (*calculated*).
* **Customer mix.** 59% of cumulative AI orders since Q1 FY2023 came from sovereign and enterprise customers [AI-05](https://investors.hpe.com/~/media/Files/H/HP-Enterprise-IR/documents/q3-2026/q3-2026-earnings-presentation.pdf); after the quarter HPE won a $3.5B inferencing deal with an unnamed hyperscaler [AI-07](https://investors.hpe.com/~/media/Files/H/HP-Enterprise-IR/documents/q3-2026/q3-2026-earnings-presentation.pdf). The 10-K warns that AI demand is lumpy and historically concentrated in a small number of large customers [AI-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1645590/000164559025000130/hpe-20251031.htm).
* **Earlier trajectory.** FY2025 AI orders were $6.8B, with $13.4B cumulative since Q1 FY2023 [AI-11](https://investors.hpe.com/~/media/Files/H/HP-Enterprise-IR/documents/q4-2025/q4-earnings-transcript-final.pdf); cumulative AI systems bookings reached $16.4B by Q2 FY2026 [AI-09](https://investors.hpe.com/~/media/Files/H/HP-Enterprise-IR/documents/q2-2026/q2-2026-transcript.pdf).

### 1.5 What HPE's own disclosures imply for an enterprise measuring AI value

1. **HPE itself names the risk this project addresses.** Its 10-K risk factors include failing to recoup AI investments [AI-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1645590/000164559025000130/hpe-20251031.htm). The same question applies to buyers.
2. **Deployment model changes the cost structure.** GreenLake consumption makes AI infrastructure a variable cost; Private Cloud AI makes it capacity you own, where **utilisation** decides unit economics. The command centre therefore tracks GPU-hours against installed capacity.
3. **Vendor efficiency claims need local evidence.** HPE's "up to 60% lower token cost" versus public cloud is HPE's own analysis [AI-24](https://investors.hpe.com/~/media/Files/H/HP-Enterprise-IR/documents/q3-2026/q3-2026-transcript.pdf). The scenario engine does not use it; it uses the enterprise's metered cost per GPU-hour.
4. **Supply and energy are real constraints.** The 10-Q discusses memory supply and pricing [AI-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1645590/000164559026000080/hpe-20260731.htm); the 10-K links AI compute to energy demand [AI-39](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1645590/000164559025000130/hpe-20251031.htm). Capacity bought ahead of demand is expensive when supply is tight and power is costly.

## Part 2. What the command centre finds in the portfolio

> SYNTHETIC: demonstration data for a fictional enterprise, not HPE or any company's actual data. The enterprise, Meridian Enterprise Group, is fictional; it runs its AI programme on HPE portfolio categories. None of these figures are HPE's. Every number below is produced by the engines and can be reproduced with `python -m scripts.build_deep_dive`.

### 2.1 The answer to "Are we actually getting value from AI?"

**Partly, and less than the business case says.** FY2026 investment was **$42.3M** (+3.3% vs budget). **$30.3M** of value is realised with evidence, **61%** of the **$49.8M** expected. In-year realised ROI is **-28%** (expected 18%), or $0.72 of value per $1. A further $8.5M is confirmed by owners but not yet in actuals, and $11.0M is still an assumption.

In-year ROI understates a programme in its first year. On a forecast basis (two more years at FY2026 exit run-rates, documented assumptions A-30 to A-32) the portfolio has a three-year NPV of $52.8M; that figure is a forecast and depends on those assumptions.

### 2.2 Where the money goes

* By cost group: Infrastructure $13.1M (31%), People $10.4M (25%), Services $6.1M (15%), Software $5.5M (13%), Cloud $5.1M (12%), Other $2.1M (5%).
* By HPE category: HPE Private Cloud AI $20.5M invested, $13.7M realised (60% of expected); HPE GreenLake cloud $8.7M invested, $5.5M realised (51% of expected); AI Infrastructure $6.6M invested, $7.6M realised (81% of expected); Networking $6.5M invested, $3.6M realised (51% of expected).
* 5 initiatives on HPE Private Cloud AI hold the largest share of investment; the category's realisation is held back mainly by two initiatives, the delayed Supply Chain AI Optimisation and the low-adoption Enterprise Knowledge Copilot.

### 2.3 How much value is real, and how much is in the P&L

* 72% of realised benefit ($23.4M) is GL-evidenced and visible in the P&L; $9.3M is operational (hours released) and becomes financial only if capacity is redeployed or cost removed. Only 45% of the business-case benefit is in the P&L today.
* Evidence quality differs sharply by benefit type: revenue uplift 59/100 (Medium); cost savings 91/100 (High); productivity value 66/100 (Medium); risk-related value 51/100 (Medium). Cost savings are finance-validated and GL-backed; revenue uplift relies on attribution models; risk value on management estimates.

### 2.4 Why realised value is below expectation (benefits leakage)

The $19.4M shortfall against the $52.1M business case decomposes exactly into:

| Cause | $M |
|---|---:|
| Pending validation | 8.5 |
| Low adoption | 5.5 |
| Delayed implementation | 3.1 |
| Incorrect assumptions | 2.4 |
| Technical constraints | 0.5 |
| Benefit measurement problem | 0.1 |
| Over-delivery vs business case | -0.6 |

* **Pending validation** is the largest item: value the business says exists but Finance has not yet evidenced. It is a measurement and recognition problem as much as a delivery problem.
* **Low adoption** and **delayed implementation** are delivery problems, concentrated in Supply Chain AI Optimisation ($5.4M, mainly low adoption), AI Revenue Intelligence ($3.1M, mainly pending validation), Enterprise Knowledge Copilot ($2.7M, mainly low adoption).
* Business run costs exceeded plan by $180K, which reduces net value further.

### 2.5 Why ROI fell this quarter

Quarterly ROI moved from 27.7% in FY26-Q3 to 13.2% in FY26-Q4 (-14.5 pp): cost effect -9.2 pp, value effect -5.3 pp. The contributions below sum exactly to the change.

| Initiative | Contribution (pp) | Value change | Cost change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AI Revenue Intelligence | -6.5 | -$546K | $92K |
| Supply Chain AI Optimisation | -4.5 | $251K | $556K |
| AI Developer Productivity | -2.0 | -$163K | $33K |
| Enterprise Knowledge Copilot | -1.3 | -$39K | $71K |
| AI Sales Forecasting | -0.9 | -$101K | -$7K |
| Cybersecurity AI | -0.9 | -$98K | -$6K |

* Infrastructure cost rose 21% quarter on quarter while GPU utilisation fell from 70.8% to 63.1%; installed GPU capacity grew 19%.
* AI Revenue Intelligence is the largest drag: its value per active user fell 55% after the cross-sell assumption was revised (A-24). Supply Chain AI Optimisation's spend rose $556K in the quarter, mostly GPU cost for added capacity, while its adoption is 57% of target after a 2-month go-live delay.

### 2.6 Cost growth, utilisation and unit economics

* Q4 spend grew 7.8% to $10.2M; the largest movements: Supply Chain AI Optimisation GPU +$376K, Supply Chain AI Optimisation Compute +$107K, AI Revenue Intelligence Cloud +$95K.
* Cluster utilisation in Q4: GreenLake AI compute APJ 83% (12 GPUs, 4,591 idle GPU-hours); GreenLake AI compute Americas 78% (12 GPUs, 5,726 idle GPU-hours); Private AI cluster EMEA 54% (28 GPUs, 28,466 idle GPU-hours); Private AI cluster Americas 53% (24 GPUs, 24,571 idle GPU-hours); Public cloud GPU burst 72% (10 GPUs, 6,088 idle GPU-hours).
* Unit economics: $2,429 per employee, $3.33 per AI interaction, $7.26 per transaction, $2.21 of cost per $1 of realised savings, $44.97 infrastructure cost per used GPU-hour.

### 2.7 Winners and at-risk initiatives

* **On track (5)**: AI Customer Support Copilot (90% realised, ROI 20%), AI Infrastructure Optimisation (95% realised, ROI 28%), IT Service Desk AI (91% realised, ROI 55%), Finance Close Copilot (105% realised, ROI 34%), Document Intelligence (90% realised, ROI 29%). Together they produce 63% of realised value from 35% of investment.
* **At risk (5)**: AI Sales Forecasting: 41% of expected value realised, +11% vs budget, 1 month(s) late; Supply Chain AI Optimisation: 28% of expected value realised, +16% vs budget, 2 month(s) late, 57% of target adoption; Enterprise Knowledge Copilot: 33% of expected value realised, +8% vs budget, 46% of target adoption; Cybersecurity AI: 36% of expected value realised, +7% vs budget; AI Revenue Intelligence: 27% of expected value realised, +9% vs budget.
* **Watch (2)**: Predictive Maintenance: 61% of expected value realised, 1 month(s) late; AI Developer Productivity: 52% of expected value realised.
* Status is derived by four documented rules (realisation, budget, schedule, adoption), not entered by hand.

### 2.8 Assumptions that carry the number

7 benefit lines rely on assumptions for at least 40% of their value ($21.5M of business case):

* Inventory carrying and expedite cost reduction (AI-003): 70% assumption-based; A-07 inventory reduction from demand sensing (source: Business case (vendor benchmark)); A-08 annual inventory carrying-cost rate (source: Treasury policy)

* Margin on incremental cross-sell and retained revenue (AI-010): 51% assumption-based; A-24 cross-sell conversion uplift (source: Business case (revised Q4 from 0.06)); A-25 churn reduction on flagged accounts (source: Business case)

### 2.9 Scenarios and sensitivity (illustrative)

* **Utilisation 63% → 80%**: about 127,094 GPU-hours a year of new workloads on existing capacity; incremental cost $6.7M, incremental value $9.2M, incremental ROI 37%, payback 8.8 months, and about $3.2M of capacity cost avoided.
* **Invest an additional $10M in proven use cases**: incremental value $11.8M, incremental ROI 18% (scaled from Green-initiative returns with a diminishing-return factor, A-35).
* **Downside (80% realisation, cloud +20%)**: expected net value falls to -$3.9M and ROI to -9.1%.
* Sensitivity ranking (net value swing for ±20%): Benefit realisation $20.8M, Implementation cost $16.9M, AI adoption $5.6M, Productivity improvement $5.6M, Revenue uplift $4.0M, Infrastructure utilisation (+/-10pp) $2.9M, Cloud cost $2.0M.

### 2.10 Areas management may wish to investigate

1. Convert the $8.5M of validated benefit into evidence: agree measurement with Finance, starting with revenue attribution.
2. Re-baseline business cases whose core assumption has already been revised (cross-sell uplift) or rests on a vendor benchmark (inventory reduction).
3. Gate further GPU capacity on demonstrated demand, and fill idle private capacity before buying more; of the scenarios tested, raising utilisation has the highest incremental ROI.
4. Treat adoption as a managed KPI for the knowledge copilot and developer tools; productivity hours count as value only when redeployed.
5. Scale what works: the Green initiatives return more than $1.3 of expected value per $1 and carry high evidence confidence.

## Part 3. Method and limitations

* Public HPE figures were read from SEC filings (XBRL company facts cross-checked with filing text) and HPE investor-relations documents; hpe.com itself was not reachable from the research environment, so HPE releases were taken from SEC 8-K exhibits and the NVIDIA newsroom. See `data/public/RESEARCH_NOTES.md` for every URL and unverified item.
* HPE does not disclose Private Cloud AI revenue or AI systems margins; no such figures are estimated here.
* The synthetic dataset is generated from documented design inputs (`src/synthetic/catalog.py`); conclusions such as which initiatives are at risk and why ROI moved are derived by the engines from the generated transactions, not written into the data.
* Confidence scores rate evidence quality; they are not probabilities. Scenario outputs are illustrative applications of documented assumptions.
