# Five-minute demo script

Run `make run` (or open `web/dist/ai-value-command-centre.html`), sign in as **Group CFO**. A machine-generated version of
every answer is in [`demo_transcript.md`](demo_transcript.md).

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00 | Open **Executive Command Center** | "This is the AI portfolio of a fictional enterprise built on HPE portfolio categories. All enterprise data is synthetic and labelled." |
| 00:30 | Point to the KPI tiles and the value bridge | "$42.3M invested, $30.3M realised with evidence, $49.8M expected. Realised ROI is −28% in year one; $0.72 back per dollar so far." Click **Explain** on Expected AI value: formula, components, data sources, assumptions. |
| 01:00 | **AI CFO Copilot** → "How much AI value have we actually realised?" | "The answer comes with drivers, numbers, evidence, assumptions and a confidence level. 72% of realised benefit is in the P&L." |
| 01:45 | "Why is realised value below expectation?" | "The $19.4M gap decomposes exactly: $8.5M pending validation, $5.5M low adoption, $3.1M delays. Supply Chain AI is the biggest single gap." |
| 02:30 | "Why did AI ROI decline this quarter?", then click the largest contributor | "ROI fell 14.5 points. The engine attributes it by initiative: the revenue-intelligence assumption was revised, and supply-chain GPU capacity arrived before demand." |
| 03:00 | Click **Show evidence** | "Source → calculation → assumption → confidence. The contributions sum exactly to the change, and here is the SQL." |
| 03:30 | **Scenario Simulator** → preset "Utilisation 63% → 80%" | "127,000 GPU-hours of new work on capacity we already own: incremental ROI 37%, payback under nine months. Assumptions are listed; it's illustrative." Show the tornado. |
| 04:15 | Copilot → "Prepare a CFO briefing." | "The Executive Reporting Agent assembles the briefing from the engines." |
| 05:00 | **Executive Report** | "Neutral language: management may wish to investigate… Decisions stay with people." |

Optional: sign in as the **Supply Chain BU leader** to show role-based scope, and open **Evidence & Trust** for HPE's public
financials, cited AI facts, the source registry and the audit log.
