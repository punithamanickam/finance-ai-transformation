# HPE Q3 FY2026 earnings release (September 2, 2026)

Headline: **"Record results and demand drive higher outlook for fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2027."** Sub-headlines: "Record revenue of $12.2 billion, up 34% year-over-year" and "Record operating profit, with GAAP OP up 464% and non-GAAP OP up 155% year-over-year."

## Management quotes

- Antonio Neri, president and CEO: "We delivered record revenue, orders, and profitability, fueled by surging customer demand across our portfolio ... AI is becoming a multi-year growth driver for HPE, and our differentiated portfolio positions us to capture that opportunity at scale."
- Marie Myers, EVP and CFO: "With our Q3 results and our order backlog at a record level, we are raising our financial outlook and plan to return at least 75% of free cash flow to shareholders in Q4."

## Q3 FY2026 consolidated results (three months ended July 31, 2026)

| Item | Q3 FY26 | Q3 FY25 |
|---|---|---|
| Net revenue | $12,213M | $9,136M |
| GAAP gross profit | $4,899M (40.1%) | $2,672M (29.2%) |

| Net earnings attributable to HPE | $1,540M | $305M |
| GAAP diluted EPS | $1.06 | $0.21 |
| Non-GAAP diluted EPS | $1.11 | $0.44 |
| Cash flow from operations | $1,641M | $1,305M |
| Free cash flow (non-GAAP) | $958M | $790M |

GAAP EPS of $1.06 was "above our outlook range of $0.84 - $0.89." Pretax earnings included a **$444M gain on sale of equity interest** (the remaining H3C stake). Capital returns to common shareholders were $324M.

## Segment results (new FY2026 structure)

- **Networking**: revenue $2.9B, "up 74.9% from the prior-year period, with 22.0% operating profit margin." Campus & Branch $1.4B (+31.0%), Data Center Networking $382M (+112.2%), Security $281M (+75.6%), Routing $788M (+270.0%). These growth rates are mostly from Juniper, which closed July 2, 2025.
- **Cloud & AI**: revenue $9.0B, "up 25.4% from the prior-year period, with 17.0% operating profit margin, compared to 7.0%." Server $6.8B (+35.3%), Storage $1.3B (+10.2%), Financial Services $0.9B (-0.3%).
- **Corporate Investments and Other**: revenue $278M (+3.0%), operating margin -24.1%.

Segment earnings from operations were: Networking $637M, Cloud & AI $1,539M, Corporate Investments $(67)M. Total segment earnings from operations were $2,109M.

Footnote (5) explains the realignment. Beginning in Q1 FY2026, HPE merged "the Server, Hybrid Cloud, and Financial Services business segments into a new segment named Cloud & AI" and moved Telco and Instant On into Corporate Investments and Other. Prior periods were recast.

## Nine months ended July 31, 2026

- Revenue: $32,192M (vs $24,617M)
- GAAP earnings from operations: $2,610M (vs a $(429)M loss that included $1,361M of impairment)
- Net earnings: $2,616M
- GAAP diluted EPS: $1.82
- Operating cash flow: $4,229M
- Free cash flow: $2,581M

## Outlook

- **Q4 FY2026**: revenue of $13.9B to $14.8B; GAAP diluted EPS of $1.12–$1.22; non-GAAP diluted EPS of $1.20–$1.30.
- **FY2026**: HPE is "raising its FY26 revenue growth outlook range to 34% to 37%." Networking growth is expected at 73% to 74%. GAAP diluted EPS is guided to $2.93–$3.03 and non-GAAP to $3.75–$3.85. Free cash flow is expected to be "at least $3.75 billion."
- **FY2027 framework**: revenue growth of 13% to 17%, non-GAAP EPS growth of 16% to 20%, non-GAAP operating margin of 14% to 15%, and free cash flow of "at least $5.0 billion."

## Definitions

HPE defines free cash flow as "cash flow from operations, less net capital expenditures (investments in property, plant & equipment ('PP&E') and software assets less proceeds from the sale of PP&E), and adjusted for the effect of exchange rate fluctuations on cash." All guidance figures are company forward-looking statements.
