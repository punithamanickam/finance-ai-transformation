# HPE AI Factory with NVIDIA: HPE Discover Las Vegas 2026 update

This summary is based on an NVIDIA blog post dated June 16, 2026, written for **HPE Discover Las Vegas 2026**. NVIDIA wrote it, not HPE, and HPE's own press releases for this event were not retrieved. Product timing is forward-looking.

## Summary statement

> "HPE AI Factory with NVIDIA expands with NVIDIA Vera CPU and NVIDIA Agent Toolkit for HPE Private Cloud AI — plus NVIDIA Confidential Computing and enhanced full-stack NVIDIA integration across HPE AI Factory solutions."

> "Enterprises are moving agentic AI from proof of concept to production — and the next generation of AI factories are built for the era of agents."

## HPE Private Cloud AI additions

- **Vera CPU**: "The HPE ProLiant Compute DL394 Gen12 with the NVIDIA Vera CPU will be available in 2027 with HPE Private Cloud AI, a turnkey AI factory co-engineered with NVIDIA." The blog names "The New York Stock Exchange, in collaboration with Redpanda and HPE" as an early enterprise customer exploring the Vera CPU on the DL394 Gen12.
- **NVIDIA Agent Toolkit**: "including NVIDIA Nemotron open models, the NVIDIA OpenShell secure runtime and NVIDIA NemoClaw blueprints — will be available with HPE Private Cloud AI."
- **Governance**: "HPE Private Cloud AI adds secure local agent registration, letting customers approve AI models, skills and tools against centralized governance and security policies before they run."
- **Data protection**: "New HPE Zerto Software capabilities detect rogue agent actions and use continuous data protection to rewind to a clean state."

## Rack-scale and large systems

- The blog says the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform is "ramping into full production with the NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 rack-scale system available from HPE."
- "HPE is also bringing the HPE Compute XD700 — built on NVIDIA HGX Rubin NVL8 — to the HPE AI Factory, supporting up to 128 Rubin GPUs per rack."
- For large-scale and sovereign workloads, HPE announced at NVIDIA GTC (March 2026) that it is adding NVIDIA InfiniBand options, "including NVIDIA Quantum-X800 InfiniBand with the HPE Cray Supercomputing GX5000."

## Storage for AI

> "HPE Alletra Storage MP X10000 — which achieved the foundation level of NVIDIA-Certified Storage — automatically applies metadata and governance policies to prepare unstructured data for AI pipelines, improving token throughput."

## Security and confidential computing

- "NVIDIA Confidential Computing is now available across the HPE AI Factory through HPE Services — including HPE AI Factory at Scale, HPE Sovereign AI Factory and HPE Private Cloud AI."
- "HPE ProLiant Compute DL380a achieved certification as part of the NVIDIA-Certified Systems for NVIDIA Confidential Computing program."
- NVIDIA BlueField DPUs and DOCA provide "in-silicon zero-trust policy enforcement, runtime threat detection and network encryption."

## Portfolio names and components

The post names three HPE AI Factory offerings: **HPE AI Factory at Scale, HPE Sovereign AI Factory and HPE Private Cloud AI**. It says they "are now available with NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs, NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet networking, NVIDIA BlueField-3 DPUs and NVIDIA ConnectX-8 SuperNICs." It adds that "Spectrum-X Ethernet networking is the standard for HPE AI Factory with NVIDIA."

## Partner ecosystem

"At HPE Discover this week, the Unleash AI partner program is expanding with nearly a dozen new AI software partners — including Aizen, BridgeTEK, deepset, Deliverance, Faclon Labs, Gallop, Rocket, Supervity, Thales, Trustwise and Vortiqx."

## Relationship to HPE financial disclosures

HPE's Q3 FY2026 call (September 2, 2026) described Private Cloud AI as "the AI factory that we co-engineer with NVIDIA" and reported that "PCAI orders increased triple-digits in Q3." HPE does not disclose Private Cloud AI revenue separately.
