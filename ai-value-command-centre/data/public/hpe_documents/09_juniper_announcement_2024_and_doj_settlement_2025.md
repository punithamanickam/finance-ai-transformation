# The Juniper deal: announcement and DOJ settlement

## Part 1: Announcement (January 9, 2024)

Headline: "HPE to Acquire Juniper Networks to Accelerate AI-Driven Innovation."

**Terms**: HPE and Juniper "entered a definitive agreement under which HPE will acquire Juniper in an all-cash transaction for $40.00 per share, representing an equity value of approximately $14 billion."

**Expected financial effects**: "The transaction is expected to be accretive to non-GAAP EPS and free cash flow in the first year post close." "The acquisition is expected to double HPE's networking business."

**Rationale**: "These trends, and AI specifically, will continue to be the most disruptive workloads for companies, and HPE has been aligning its portfolio to capitalize on these substantial IT trends with networking as a critical connective component."

**Mist AI**: "Through its suite of cloud-delivered networking solutions, software, and services including the Mist AI and Cloud platform, Juniper helps organizations securely and efficiently access the mission-critical cloud infrastructure that serves as the foundation of digital and AI strategies." The release says combining this with "HPE Aruba Networking and purposely designed HPE AI interconnect fabric" would create an industry player optimized "for hybrid cloud and AI."

**Leadership**: "Upon completion of the transaction, Juniper CEO Rami Rahim will lead the combined HPE networking business, reporting to HPE President and CEO Antonio Neri."

**Pro forma mix**: "the new networking segment will increase from approximately 18% of total HPE revenue as of fiscal year 2023 to approximately 31% and contribute more than 56% of HPE's total operating income." Footnotes say this uses FY2023 HPE Intelligent Edge figures and Juniper LTM figures to 09/30/23. The operating profit figure includes "run-rate cost synergies of $450 million." HPE later raised its synergy target to at least $600 million by FY2028 in its 10-K.

**GreenLake**: "Networking will become the new core business and architecture foundation for HPE's Hybrid Cloud and AI solutions delivered through our HPE GreenLake hybrid cloud platform."

**Neri quote**: "This transaction will strengthen HPE's position at the nexus of accelerating macro-AI trends, expand our total addressable market, and drive further innovation for customers as we help bridge the AI-native and cloud-native worlds."

## Part 2: DOJ settlement (June 28, 2025)

According to the FY2025 10-K, the U.S. Department of Justice filed suit on January 30, 2025 to block the merger. On June 28, 2025, HPE and Juniper announced they had "reached an agreement with the U.S. Department of Justice ('DOJ') that, subject to court approval, resolves the DOJ's lawsuit challenging HPE's acquisition of Juniper and clears the way for the transaction to close."

**Remedies**:

> "As part of the settlement, HPE has agreed to divest its global Instant On campus and branch business. After close, HPE will facilitate limited access to Juniper's advanced Mist AIOps technology."

**Neri quote**: "For the first time, customers will now have a modern network architecture alternative that can best support the demands of AI workloads. The combination of HPE Aruba Networking and Juniper Networks will provide customers with a comprehensive portfolio of secure, AI-native networking solutions, and accelerate HPE's ability to grow in the AI data center, service provider and cloud segments."

**About Juniper (as stated in the release)**: "Mist™, Juniper's AI-native networking platform is purpose-built to run AI workloads and simplify IT operations."

## Subsequent status (per later HPE disclosures)

- The deal closed July 2, 2025, with cash consideration of approximately $13.4 billion per the FY2025 10-K.
- From FY2026, the Instant On business was reported in Corporate Investments and Other.
- HPE said on its Q3 FY2026 call that "In August, a US Federal court approved our settlement with the Department of Justice."
