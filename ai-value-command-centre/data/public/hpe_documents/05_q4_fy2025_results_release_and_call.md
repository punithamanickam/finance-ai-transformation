# HPE Q4 FY2025 results and call (December 4, 2025)

## Earnings release (8-K Exhibit 99.1)

Headline: "HPE reports fiscal 2025 fourth quarter results — Posts record quarterly revenue and gross profit; raises FY26 guidance."

Antonio Neri: "During the year, we completed the Juniper Networks acquisition, further scaled our AI and Cloud businesses, and accelerated innovation across our portfolio."

Q4 FY2025 highlights as stated in the release:
- "Revenue: $9.7 billion, up 14% from the prior-year period"
- "Annualized revenue run-rate ('ARR'): $3.2 billion, up 63% from the prior-year period in actual dollars and 62% in constant currency"
- GAAP gross margin 33.5%; non-GAAP gross margin 36.4%
- GAAP diluted EPS $0.11; non-GAAP diluted EPS $0.62
- Cash flow from operations $2.5B; free cash flow $1.9B

Q4 FY2025 segment results (old five-segment structure):
- Server revenue: $4.5B, down 5%, with 9.8% operating margin
- Networking revenue: $2.8B, up 150%, with 23% operating margin
- Hybrid Cloud revenue: $1.4B, down 12%, with 5% operating margin
- Financial Services revenue: $889M, flat, with 11.5% operating margin and return on equity of 20.8%

ARR definition (release footnote): "ARR represents the annualized revenue of all net HPE GreenLake cloud services revenue, related financial services revenue ... and software-as-a-Service, software consumption revenue, and other as-a-Service offerings, by taking such revenue recognized during a quarter and multiplying by four." Starting with the quarter ended July 31, 2025, the calculation also includes software license support and maintenance revenue, to align with Juniper.

FY2026 guidance at the time: revenue growth of 17% to 22%, non-GAAP diluted EPS of $2.25–$2.45, and free cash flow of $1.7B–$2.0B. HPE raised this guidance several times during FY2026 (see the Q3 FY2026 release).

## Earnings call: AI and cloud metrics

- **AI orders**: "our AI server business also had good traction with orders totaling $6.8 billion for the fiscal year and cumulative AI orders since Q1 fiscal 2023 reaching $13.4 billion."
- **Q4 AI systems orders** (CFO): "Turning to AI systems, orders were strong in the fourth quarter, reaching $1.9 billion, largely fueled by demand for sovereign customers." Myers added: "we expect AI demand to remain uneven as some of our larger sovereign customers are placing orders with extended lead times, which may defer shipments to future periods."
- **Customer mix**: "Sovereign and enterprise bookings now account for more than 60% of the cumulative orders since Q1 of fiscal year '23."
- **GreenLake**: "We added approximately 7,000 new customers to GreenLake, ending the year with approximately 46,000 customers."
- **ARR**: "Total company ARR was $3.2 billion, up 62% year-over-year with the addition of Juniper," and "unique cloud-native software, AIOps and services ... together represented over 80% of ARR." The release says 63% in actual dollars and 62% in constant currency, which explains the small difference.
- **Alletra MP**: "We have now shipped over 7,400 Alletra MP Storage arrays, more than doubling year-over-year."
- **Private Cloud AI**: "we are also seeing good growth in private cloud AI orders, which more than doubled sequentially, and we closed the year with approximately 100 new logos in this space."
- Private cloud software named on the call: HPE Morpheus Enterprise, VM Essentials, the Cloud Ops suite, Zerto and OpsRamp. Management described ARR as combining networking SaaS such as Aruba Central and Juniper Mist, hybrid cloud software, and Alletra MP storage.
