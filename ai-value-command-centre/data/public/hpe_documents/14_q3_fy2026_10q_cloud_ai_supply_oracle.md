# HPE Q3 FY2026 Form 10-Q: AI-relevant disclosures

## Segment structure

"Effective November 1, 2025, HPE implemented an organizational change by (i) merging the Server, Hybrid Cloud, and Financial Services business segments into a new segment named Cloud & Artificial Intelligence ('AI') and (ii) transferring the Telco and Instant On businesses from the Networking segment to the Corporate Investments and Other segment." HPE now has three segments: Cloud & AI, Networking, and Corporate Investments and Other. The CODM is "Antonio F. Neri, who is the President and Chief Executive Officer."

The 10-Q describes Cloud & AI as including "server and storage offerings." The server portfolio includes "integrated systems comprised of software and hardware designed to address High-Performance Computing and Supercomputing (including exascale applications), AI, Data Analytics, and Transaction Processing workloads."

## Consolidated results commentary

For the three months: "Net revenue of $12.2 billion represented an increase of 33.7%, primarily due to an increase in the average selling price in the Cloud & AI segment, and higher revenue in the Networking segment from the Merger. The gross profit margin of 40.1% (or $4.9 billion) represents an increase of 10.9 percentage points."

For the nine months: net revenue of $32.2 billion, up 30.8%, with gross margin of 37.7%.

## Cloud & AI drivers (three months ended July 31, 2026)

- "Cloud & AI segment net revenue increased by $1.8 billion, or 25.4%, primarily due to an increase in Server net revenue. Server net revenue increased by $1.8 billion, or 35.3%, predominantly due to an increase in the average selling price. The increase in average selling price was primarily driven by commodity price increases, especially memory and SSDs."
- "Cloud & AI gross profit increased by $1.5 billion, or 89.8%, primarily driven by increased net revenue. This increase was due to favorable mix of higher-margin revenues."
- "Cloud & AI earnings from operations increased by $1.0 billion, or 205.4%, primarily driven by higher gross profit, partially offset by an increase in operating expenses of $0.5 billion, or 40.2%, primarily due to higher variable employee costs."

Cloud & AI net revenue was $9,042M for the quarter (vs $7,212M) and $23,083M for the nine months (vs $19,994M).

## AI-driven supply constraints

> "We have experienced supply chain constraints for certain components, including graphics processing units ('GPUs'), accelerated processing units, solid-state drives ('SSDs'), and other memory components. We are affected by the worldwide shortage in memory components that began to impact the semiconductor industry throughout fiscal year 2026 primarily due to the accelerating growth in AI usage and demand and the related rapid expansion in AI data centers and compute refresh cycles."

HPE says it expects "such dynamics to continue in the medium term." The 10-Q links higher days payable outstanding to "higher purchases to secure strategic component supply and fulfill backlog."

## Market trends

"We have observed market trends and demand ... gravitating towards AI, hybrid cloud, edge computing, data security capabilities, and related offerings ... Increasing demand for AI is also contributing to changes in the competitive landscape."

## Oracle warrant (AI data center networking)

> "On July 2, 2026, we issued a warrant (the 'Warrant') to Oracle Corp. ('Holder') to purchase up to an aggregate of 4,156,466 shares of our common stock at an exercise price of $0.01 per share. The Warrant was issued in connection with the Holder entering into a strategic collaboration with us to deploy our networking solutions in the Holder's AI data centers. The Warrant will vest and become exercisable in tranches, upon the achievement of specific milestones relating to purchases by the Holder and its affiliates of products and support from us."

The warrant "is exercisable through June 25, 2029." On the September 2, 2026 call, HPE described an "expanded collaboration with Oracle to accelerate gigawatt scale AI infrastructure."

## Goodwill: Cloud & AI (excluding Financial Services)

As of November 1, 2025, the former Server and Hybrid Cloud reporting units were combined into a single "Cloud & AI (excluding Financial Services)" reporting unit. An interim impairment test found fair value exceeded carrying amount by 10%. That reporting unit "has goodwill of $13.5 billion as of July 31, 2026." The 10-Q also notes that "In the second quarter of fiscal 2025, the Company recorded a goodwill impairment charge of $1.4 billion associated with the Cloud & AI (excluding Financial Services) reporting unit."
