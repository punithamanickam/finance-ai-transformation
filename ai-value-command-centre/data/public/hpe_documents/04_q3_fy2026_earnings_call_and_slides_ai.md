# HPE Q3 FY2026 call and slides: AI metrics

The call took place Wednesday, September 2, 2026 at 4:30 PM ET, with Antonio Neri (CEO), Marie Myers (CFO) and Shannon Cross (Chief Strategy Officer). HPE says verbal figures are rounded and that "Unless otherwise noted, all financial metrics and growth rates discussed today are non-GAAP." Some figures are "normalized to include Juniper Networks results as of the beginning of HPE's fiscal year 2025."

## AI systems (Cloud & AI segment)

- Orders: "AI systems orders of $2.4 billion increased over 30% sequentially, reflecting broad-based demand across customer segments." (Myers)
- Enterprise: "Enterprise demand more than doubled, reflecting increasing overall infrastructure spending as AI initiatives have become board level priorities."
- Backlog: "Our AI systems backlog increased 14% sequentially to a new high and our pipeline remains multiples of our backlog." The slide gives the figure: "$2.4B in new AI Systems orders, with backlog increasing to $6.8B."
- Revenue: "AI systems revenue for the quarter was almost $1.6 billion. We expect AI systems revenue to improve sequentially in Q4 given timing of backlog conversion."
- New deal: "after quarter end HPE was awarded a multi-billion-dollar server deal with a hyperscaler customer specifically designed for inferencing." The slide sizes it: "Post Q3, HPE was awarded a $3.5B inferencing deal with hyperscaler customer." The customer is not named.

## Total AI backlog, AI Systems plus Networks for AI (slide 9)

- "$7.6B AI backlog, Exiting Q3 FY26" (record)
- "$3.1B AI orders booked in Q3 FY26"
- "$6.7B AI YTD orders"
- "59% Sovereign + enterprise cumulative AI order mix" (cumulative since Q1 FY23)
- AI Systems backlog by quarter, from the slide chart ($B): Q4 FY24 2.4, Q1 FY25 3.1, Q2 FY25 3.2, Q3 FY25 3.7, Q4 FY25 4.7, Q1 FY26 5.0, Q2 FY26 5.9, Q3 FY26 6.8.

Slide footnotes say orders and backlog "remain subject to ongoing adjustment for a variety of reasons (including but not limited to re-bookings, cancellations, and fulfillment issues)." They also say "Networks for AI backlog tracking commenced in Q1'26" and "AI Systems include products and services. Networks for AI does not include services."

## Networks for AI (Networking segment)

"Networks for AI demand accelerated in Q3 with orders reaching a new high of $700 million, up triple digits." "Cumulative networks for AI orders were $2.2 billion, surpassing our FY 2026 target. As a result, we are increasing our yearend target to $2.5 billion to $3 billion." Myers cited the "direct liquid cooled Tomahawk 6 base switch" and the "PTX and MX routing portfolio." She said pipeline acceleration is expected "as we bring the Helios platform to market."

Neri announced "an expanded collaboration with Oracle to accelerate gigawatt scale AI infrastructure. Oracle will deploy HPE Juniper networking routers and switches across one of the largest AI cloud infrastructure build outs."

## Storage and HPE Private Cloud AI

- Neri: "Storage had a standout quarter with record revenue," with demand for "HPE, Alletra and MP storage solutions."
- Myers: "PCAI orders increased triple-digits in Q3 as customers are adopting our AI factory platform to support agentic AI and inferencing initiatives. Alletra MP orders and revenue increased strong double-digits year-over-year." She also cited growth potential for the "X10K object and file system."
- Neri: "Our private cloud AI platform allows customers to manage enterprise AI applications and AI agents while keeping control of data governance, security and operations."
- Token-cost claim (Myers): "According to our own internal analysis, our PCAI offering can reduce token costs versus the public cloud by up to 60%." This is a company claim. Slide 14's footnote lists the assumptions, including a 5-year term, 80% utilization and an 8B-parameter model. It has not been independently verified.
- Internal use: "HPE is now deploying an internal agentic AI platform built on our own private cloud AI."

## GreenLake and Financial Services

"In Q3, the number of GreenLake customers grew 18% to 52,000 up from 44,000 a year ago." Neri said HPE Financial Services "generated third quarter records in financing volumes, residual value and return on equity." Myers said Financial Services revenue "was roughly flat year-over-year" and that return on equity exceeded 20%.

## Supply constraints and pricing

Neri: "Supply constraints continue to affect our ability to fulfill the increased customer demand." Myers attributed server revenue growth of 35% to "strong ASP growth in traditional service offset supply constrained unit volumes." The transcript text reads "traditional service" in several places, most likely a transcription of "traditional servers." She also said "Traditional server orders up 75% year-over-year" (slide). Inventory ended the quarter at $11.8B.

## Guidance comments

For Q4, Cloud & AI revenue is expected "to grow 60% to 72%." Operating margin is expected to moderate "to a mid-teens rate." The FY2027 framework calls for Cloud & AI operating margin of "approximately 13%" and EPS of "$4.40 to $4.60." Myers said the Juniper synergy capture "remains on track to achieve our $600 million annual run rate savings target by the end of FY 2028."
