# HPE closes the Juniper Networks acquisition (July 2, 2025)

Subheadline: "Combination accelerates HPE's strategic vision with a full, secure networking IP stack."

## Announcement

> "HOUSTON – July 2, 2025 – HPE (NYSE: HPE) today announced the successful completion of its previously announced acquisition of Juniper Networks, Inc., a leader in AI-native networks. The combination positions HPE to capture the growing AI and hybrid cloud market opportunity by creating an industry-leading cloud-native and AI-driven IT portfolio, including a full, modern networking stack."

> "The transaction doubles the size of HPE's networking business and provides customers with a comprehensive portfolio of networking solutions."

## Quotes

- Antonio Neri: "Today begins a new era for HPE – we are now at the epicenter of the transformation of IT, where AI and networking are converging."
- Rami Rahim, former Juniper CEO, who now leads HPE Networking: "Together, we'll be able to provide customers and partners with a secure network that is purpose-built with AI and for AI."

## Stated strategic and financial benefits

- **Full networking IP stack**: "from silicon, to hardware, to the operating system, to security, to software and services, with a cloud-native and AI-driven approach."
- **Networking scale**: the deal "doubles the size of HPE's networking business." It extends HPE into "data center, firewalls, and routers," combining HPE's enterprise networking and SASE strength "with Juniper's position in data center, service provider, and AI-native solutions."
- **AI-native foundation**: modern networking for "data-intensive, hybrid AI workloads," with "Greater research and development scale" across "networking silicon, systems, and software."
- **Financial**: "The transaction will be accretive to non-GAAP EPS in year 1, post close, with the combined networking business contributing more than 50% of total company operating income."

The release adds that the acquisition "was originally announced on January 9, 2024, and was approved by Juniper shareholders on April 2, 2024." JNPR shares ceased trading on the NYSE on the closing date.

## Consideration (from HPE's FY2025 10-K, Note on acquisitions)

> "On July 2, 2025, the Company completed the Juniper Networks, Inc. ('Juniper Networks') merger ... HPE agreed to pay $40.00 per share of Juniper Networks common stock ... representing a cash consideration of approximately $13.4 billion."

The January 2024 announcement described an equity value of "approximately $14 billion." The FY2025 10-K cash consideration figure is approximately $13.4 billion. The two figures measure slightly different things, so use the 10-K figure for accounting purposes.

The 10-K also states that "the Company expects to achieve at least $600 million in cost savings from synergies by fiscal 2028, related to the integration of Juniper Networks. These synergies will require approximately $800 million of investment."

## Post-close developments (from later HPE disclosures)

- Rami Rahim "has served as Executive Vice President, President and General Manager of our Networking business since he joined HPE in July 2025" (FY2025 10-K).
- The FY2025 Networking segment includes Juniper from July 2, 2025. The 10-K attributes $1,367M of FY2025 product revenue growth and $729M of service revenue growth to Juniper.
- Q3 FY2026 call (September 2, 2026): "In August, a US Federal court approved our settlement with the Department of Justice." Neri said "our integration plan and cost synergies remain ahead of schedule."
- Mist: the FY2025 10-K lists "Mist and Aruba Central for cloud-based and on-premise management" among Networking software products. The DOJ settlement required HPE to "facilitate limited access to Juniper's advanced Mist AIOps technology" (see the separate settlement document).
