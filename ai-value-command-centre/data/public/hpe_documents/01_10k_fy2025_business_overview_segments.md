# HPE FY2025 10-K — Business overview and segments

## Strategy

HPE's FY2025 annual report frames its strategy around three "megatrends": networking, cloud and AI. The 10-K states that HPE has "evolved our business to a platform-based model, fueled by a portfolio that is richer in software and services."

On **networking**, the filing says the AI market "requires a modern and high-performing networking fabric" and that HPE intends to drive "'AI for Networks'— which we define as using AI to make networks smarter — and 'Networks for AI'— which builds the foundational infrastructure for next-generation AI applications." It adds that the acquisition of Juniper Networks, Inc. gives HPE "a full networking technology stack."

On **cloud**, the filing calls HPE GreenLake cloud "a centerpiece of our strategy" and says it "is designed to deliver a seamless hybrid experience with true pay-per-use consumption and automated scalability." HPE also says it "is driving a transition to proprietary HPE-developed storage solutions that enhance profitability and reduce reliance on third-party products."

On **AI**, the 10-K states: "In AI infrastructure, HPE is focused on sovereign and enterprise customers, leveraging our supercomputing heritage and turnkey AI factory solutions, including HPE Private Cloud AI and scalable AI servers." It cites "technologies like fanless direct liquid cooling that power our largest AI systems" and "our market-leading supercomputing portfolio of HPE Cray EX solutions and systems."

The filing says the financial services business "offers customers the ability to accelerate adoption of AI through financing options from us as well as through IT life cycle services."

## Reportable segments in FY2025

In FY2025 HPE reported five segments: **Server, Hybrid Cloud, Networking, Financial Services, and Corporate Investments and Other.** Product classes above 10% of consolidated revenue in FY2025 were "Server products, Networking products." One distributor made up about 10% of total net revenue in FY2025.

- **Server**: general-purpose and workload-optimized servers and "integrated systems ... designed to address High-Performance Computing and Supercomputing (including exascale applications), AI, Data Analytics, and Transaction Processing workloads." Named products: HPE ProLiant, HPE Synergy, HPE Scale Up Servers, HPE Edgeline, HPE Cray EX, HPE Cray XD (formerly HPE Apollo) and HPE NonStop.
- **Hybrid Cloud**: storage (the "HPE Alletra Storage portfolio; unstructured data solutions and analytics for AI"), AIOps tools HPE InfoSight and HPE CloudPhysics, HPE GreenLake private cloud offerings including "a portfolio of world-class Private Cloud AI infrastructure delivered as-a-service," HPE GreenLake for Private Cloud Business Edition with VM Essentials, OpsRamp observability, HPE Ezmeral Data Fabric, and Zerto disaster recovery.
- **Networking**: Wi-Fi and private cellular access points, QFX/EX/CX switches, MX/PTX routers, gateways, plus "software products, such as Mist and Aruba Central," SD-WAN, network security and services.
- **Financial Services**: leasing, financing, IT consumption and asset management. It "also supports financial solutions for on-premise flexible consumption models, such as the HPE GreenLake cloud."
- **Corporate Investments and Other**: "the Advisory and Professional Services business, which primarily offers consultative-led services ... and Hewlett Packard Labs."

Consolidated FY2025 net revenue was $34,296M, up 13.8%. HPE attributed the increase "primarily due to higher revenue in the Networking segment from the Merger and higher average unit prices ('AUPs') in the Server segment." Server gross profit fell 8.3% "due to the input cost increases and higher mix of lower margin products." Hybrid Cloud gross profit fell "as we transition to a more software-defined platform with HPE Alletra."

## Segment realignment for FY2026

The 10-K discloses that from the first quarter of FY2026 HPE "implemented an organizational change by (i) merging the Server, Hybrid Cloud, and Financial Services business segments into a new segment named Cloud & AI and (ii) transferring the Telco and Instant On businesses from the Networking segment to the Corporate Investments and Other segment." From FY2026 the segments are Networking, Cloud & AI, and Corporate Investments and Other.

## R&D

"Expenditures for R&D were $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025, $2.2 billion fiscal 2024, and $2.3 billion in fiscal 2023." In Hybrid Cloud AI, HPE says it has "invested in building turn-key integrated systems co-engineered with Nvidia ... optimized for inferencing, retrieval augmented generation, and model fine-tuning." In servers, R&D covers "leading edge silicon architectures, high-speed interconnects, advanced storage and innovative direct liquid cooling."
